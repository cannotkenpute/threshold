## 📁 ACT I: DISCOVERY & PRELIMINARY INVESTIGATION (1981 – 1986)

### **May 1981 — Initial Spatial Breach**
* **The Anomaly Detected:** Department of Spatial Anomaly (DSA) field researchers detect an anomalous non-Euclidean rift behind the sub-basement drywall of an ordinary federal commercial building.
* **Unidentified Substance #29:** Preliminary reconnaissance teams extract a glowing green, self-illuminating organic compound exhibiting anomalous atomic decay. The material is sealed inside a cylindrical pressurized containment vessel within the Level 5 Security Staging Annex.

## 📁 ACT II: PROJECT THRESHOLD & EXPEDITION DISASTERS (1987 – 1988)

### **08 October 1987 — Spatial Environment Confirmation (`Document 01`)**
* Research Division personnel officially confirm that the interior volume of the breach exceeds the physical dimensions of the entry facility by several orders of magnitude, occupying no measurable geographic coordinates.
* **11 October 1987:** The project is codified as **`PROJECT THRESHOLD`** and stamped **`TOP SECRET // CLASSIFIED`**.

### **Late October 1987 — Permanent Access Program (`Document 02`)**
* The Spatial Survey Division authorizes permanent research infrastructure beyond Threshold Point A.
* Handwritten directive from command: *“Vaughn wants Phase II operational before January.”*
* Supervisor Kenneth Vaughn issues initial recorded briefing: *“Your assignment is straightforward—”* (`v1.mp3`).

### **November 1987 — Public Suspicions & Midnight Convoy (`Newspaper Clipping 01`)**
* Residents near the facility report midnight convoys of unmarked military trucks, heavy excavation equipment, and *“industrial machinery running underground.”* Federal officials decline all comment.

### **December 1987 — Expedition Team 04 Disappearance (`Document 03`)**
* **Expedition Team 04** is deployed deep into the Yellow Sector.
* At **02:17**, all telemetry and radio contact abruptly flatline.
* All five members (Lead Researcher, Biological Survey, Security, Engineering, Communications) are officially designated **`MISSING`**.
* Overlapping radio transmissions echo Vaughn’s warnings: *“—we lost contact at 02:17—”* (`v2.mp3`) and *“Do not deviate from the marked route.”* (`v3.mp3`).

### **January 1988 — Mandatory Safety Directive (`Document 04`)**
* Strict safety bulletin issued: All personnel entering the anomaly must stay physically connected to a motorized steel retrieval line.
* *“UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES SHOULD THE TETHER BE REMOVED. FOLLOW THE TETHER BACK TO THRESHOLD POINT A.”*
* Vaughn’s voice: *“The tether is your only way back.”* (`v4.mp3`) / *“—you are not authorized to investigate—”* (`v5.mp3`).

### **14 March 1988 — Survey Corridor 6 Photograph (`Polaroid #06`)**
* Reconnaissance sweep develops a grainy instant photograph of a yellow hallway stretching beyond flash range, with a circled dark humanoid silhouette in the distance (*SURVEY CORRIDOR 6 — 03/14/88*).

### **Spring 1988 — Traumatized Retrieval Interview (`Document 05`)**
* A solitary retrieved subject exhibits severe disorientation, unable to recognize recovery team members. At 04:31, the subject begins repeating: **“THERE WAS SOMETHING BEHIND US.”**
* Interviews are immediately suppressed under a red **`DO NOT DISTRIBUTE`** stamp.

### **Mid 1988 — Phase III Construction & Subject Procurement (`Documents 06 & 07`)**
* **Phase III Expansion:** Full-scale infrastructure authorized for a permanent population of **312** (housing, medical bays, power grid, communications relay, civilian holding blocks).
* **Civilian Procurement:** Disappearances across three neighboring counties escalate. Unofficial research authorizations increase civilian exposure durations (*“psychological deterioration considered acceptable”*). Stamped with handwritten approval: *“V. APPROVED.”*

### **Late 1988 — Threshold Failure & Facility Evacuation**
* Spatial shifts destabilize the entry perimeter. A cascading **`THRESHOLD FAILURE`** triggers full facility lockdown and emergency evacuation orders.

### **1. The Final Authorization & Gateway Deployment**
* **Recovery Mission Authorized:** The Department of Spatial Anomaly authorizes a solo replacement researcher to locate Expedition Team 04 via physical tether.
* Supervisor Vaughn delivers the final cold radio command: **“Proceed.”** (`v8.mp3`).

### **2. Level 1: The Yellow Sector (Threshold A)**
* **Tether Failure:** After venturing into the non-Euclidean labyrinth, the physical tether snaps into slack air. Upon returning, the doorway has vanished, replaced by solid drywall.
* **Recovered Audio Tapes:**
  - *Dr. Mercer Log 01 (09:42)*: Checkpoint Alpha established ($400\text{m}$ in).
  - *Daniel Cole Log 02 (11:15)*: Footsteps pacing behind the team on silent carpet (*"Carpet doesn't echo"*).
  - *Dr. Mercer Final Log (17:00)*: Discovery that the mimic entity is imitating her voice to hunt survivors behind the steel barricade (*"That isn't me"*).
* **Vaughn's Resignation:** The player unlocks the Level 5 Security Annex, discovering Substance #29 and Supervisor Vaughn’s signed resignation letter—revealing command abandoned the post before the player ever stepped inside.
* **Descent:** Breaching the maintenance barricade, the operative plunges down the descent hatch into Sub-Level 2.

### **3. Level 2: Subterranean Parking Garage**
* **The Car Cemetery:** The operative enters an infinite underground concrete parking complex of abandoned civilian vehicles without license plates.
* **Dr. Mercer's Garage Tape:** Found on the hood of a burgundy sedan, documenting the team attempting to hotwire an escape vehicle before scattering.
* **Vehicular Breakout:** The operative recovers a crowbar and an emergency gasoline canister, jimmies the locks of an intact sedan, refuels the dry tank, and accelerates through the perimeter tunnel into the dark.

### **4. Level 3: Route 9 (The Infinite Nocturnal Highway)**
* **The 10-Hour Drive:** The vehicle breaks through the garage perimeter onto **Route 9**, an infinite, fog-drenched two-lane highway surrounded by towering dark pines.
* **Officer Alvarez Dispatch (Unit 412):** At repeating mile marker `MILE 412`, the operative discovers a flashing State Highway Patrol cruiser containing Officer Alvarez's dashboard tape (*"Mile markers keep repeating... do not pull over into the fog"*).
* **The Abandoned Gas Station & Store:** Deep along the highway shoulder lies an abandoned roadside gas station and convenience store containing the final cassette log, cementing the realization that the anomaly spans far beyond the initial facility walls.
