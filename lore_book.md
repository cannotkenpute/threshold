# PART I: THE STORY SO FAR

### Chapter 1: The Discovery of Anomaly DTE-04 (May 1981)
In the spring of 1981, Department of Spatial Anomaly researchers detected a localized non-Euclidean rift behind the sub-basement drywall of an ordinary commercial building. Initial sweeps revealed an impossibly vast interior space consisting of repeating mono-yellow wallpaper, damp carpet, and humming fluorescent ballasts. During preliminary sweeps, researchers extracted an unknown luminescent green organic compound—designated **Unidentified Substance #29**—which exhibited anomalous atomic decay and was sealed inside a cylindrical containment tank in the Level 5 Security Staging Annex.

### Chapter 2: The Mercer Expedition (October 1982)
In October 1982, a 4-person deep-reconnaissance scientific team was dispatched across the threshold to map the structure:
* **Dr. Evelyn Mercer** — Lead Physicist & Field Director
* **Dr. Samuel Reed** — Spatial Topologist & Mathematician
* **Daniel Cole** — Comms & Lifeline Tether Engineer
* **Dr. Helen Park** — Biologist & Medical Specialist

The team was connected to the staging facility via a heavy steel-reinforced tether cable anchored to an automated motorized winch.

At first, the expedition appeared routine (**Expedition Log 01**). However, within hours, Euclidean physics began collapsing. Dr. Samuel Reed discovered that walking north and making three consecutive left turns brought the team back to the exact same wall marking. Furthermore, compass needles ceased pointing toward Earth’s magnetic north, instead aligning with the nearest ceiling fluorescent light ballasts.

### Chapter 3: The Metamorphosis & The Mimic Entity
Shortly after establishing Checkpoint Alpha, the expedition made two terrifying discoveries:
1. **The Biological Reality**: Dr. Helen Park’s chemical assays on the damp carpet padding and peeling wallpaper revealed that the structure was not inert construction drywall, but an active, metabolizing cellular organism reacting to body heat and vibration.
2. **The Stalker ("The Voice")**: Daniel Cole noticed footsteps keeping pace with him in the dark. Because wet wool carpet does not produce acoustic echoes, the sounds were proven to be a secondary entity stalking behind them.

When the team attempted to backtrack, they discovered that **the gateway entrance had vanished**. The heavy steel tether had not snapped; it simply terminated into empty space as the geometry reorganized. Desperate, the team barricaded themselves inside a maintenance shelter behind a reinforced steel security door. Outside in the dark, the entity began mimicking Dr. Mercer’s own voice (*"Evelyn... Evelyn..."*) using their chalk markers to hunt them down.

### Chapter 4: Supervisor Vaughn’s Desertion
Back in the real world at the staging facility, **Supervisor Kenneth Vaughn** monitored the telemetry links as the expedition’s signals flatlined and the gateway winch went slack. Realizing that the anomaly was expanding inward toward the real world and that Mercer's team was irrecoverable, Vaughn panicked.

Bound by cold bureaucratic protocol, Vaughn recorded an automated radio dispatch (`dialogue#1.mp3`) on the gateway frequency ordering a solo rescue operative into the portal. He then drafted an **Official Notice of Resignation**, abandoned his post, and fled the facility before perimeter seals were locked down.

### Chapter 5: The Solo Operative (The Player's Journey)
You awaken in the staging laboratory. Vaughn’s recorded radio transmission plays, giving you the illusion of mission command support. You step across the threshold into the Yellow Sector.

As you explore, you locate Dr. Mercer’s early audio logs and keycard credentials. Unlocking the Level 5 Security Staging Annex reveals the devastating truth: the glowing containment tube holding Substance #29 and Vaughn’s signed resignation letter on the central desk, proving you were abandoned before you ever took your first step.

With the gateway gone, you breach the final maintenance barricade at the end of Level 1 and locate Mercer’s bloodstained final cassette tape. Finding a descent hatch marked *"LEVEL 2? / DON'T GO BACK"*, you plunge into the sub-levels.

### Chapter 6: Level 2 — The Subterranean Parking Garage
You emerge into an endless underground parking garage of damp concrete pillars, low 2.8m ceilings, yellow hazard stripes, and paradoxical illuminated exit signs. 

Scattered across the parking bays are dozens of locked 1980s civilian vehicles with missing license plates. On the hood of an abandoned burgundy sedan at $(0, 0)$, you recover Dr. Mercer’s garage audio log. Mercer’s team had attempted to hotwire an escape vehicle before scattering in search of tools and fuel.

Scavenging the dark corridors, you locate a steel crowbar in a maintenance tool cache and an emergency fuel canister between parked cars. You jimmy the lock of an intact getaway sedan, pour the gasoline into the dry tank, and turn the key. The engine roars to life, and you floor the accelerator through the concrete tunnel into the darkness.

### Chapter 7: Level 3 — Route 9 (The Infinite Dark Highway)
Crashing through the garage perimeter, you find yourself on an infinite two-lane weathered asphalt highway—**Route 9 North**—shrouded in thick fog and hemmed in by towering dark pine forests. 

A continuous Day/Night cycle shifts the world between sickly overcast twilight and pitch-black midnight where only sodium-vapor streetlamps and headlights pierce the mist. Abandoned civilian vehicles and repeating green mile markers (`MILE 412`) line the shoulders.

At Mile 412, you discover an abandoned State Highway Patrol cruiser with its red and blue roof beacons still flashing. On the front seat lies the dash tape of **Officer J. Alvarez (Unit 412)**, who was trapped in the same spatial loop after driving for hours on a road that never ends...
